# Delivery Review Dispatch

For the delivering parent only, after the global review gate is triggered. Review subagents use `delivery-review` instead. This skill neither authorizes other delegation nor makes routine low-risk work reviewable.

## Handoff before release

For consequential work, before each answer/revision reread task-relevant original evidence and the full prior candidate/BLOCK history; reassess the goal, constraints, authority, dependencies, options and effects; identify the smallest complete deliverable for the user's actual use point and its decisive questions before drafting or repairing. In a writable task workspace keep one append-only `.delivery-review-history.md` across turns (outside delivery and commits). Before every review append the complete current `DELIVERABLE` (full text, or an immutable accessible full version for non-text artifacts) and a brief `PARENT EXPLANATION`: a checkable evidence map for its decisive claims and choices, covering original evidence, material competing interpretations, assumptions, inferences or counterexamples, limits, authority, unresolved dependencies and affected dependents, including unchanged grounds when still material. Do not write point-by-point defenses against prior reviews, narrate the drafting process, or add auxiliary analyses merely because they could be reviewed; repair the artifact and cite only grounds needed to assess it and applicable BLOCKs. After review append the reviewer's verbatim `PASS BAR`, verdict, defects, repairs and release status. Never overwrite; if inaccessible disclose the gap and consult available chat history. Exclude secrets and needless private data.

Review one coherent, unreleased candidate before delivery; retrospective critique cannot retract prior delivery. Use one dispatch per review attempt, containing only:

- `RAW EVIDENCE`: in the dispatch message, reproduce every task-relevant user turn verbatim and in order, including corrections and superseded facts, plus task-relevant original source, test and authorization records. Do not include parent drafts, interpretations, review history or proposed verdicts. Label redactions, unavailable material or source gaps; exclude unrelated history, unapproved secrets and unnecessary private data.
- `HISTORY FILE`: give the exact accessible path and current turn/revision in `.delivery-review-history.md`. It holds complete current artifacts, candidate interpretations and prior reviews as review material, not original evidence; do not paste their contents into the dispatch.

Include `你是交付审核子智能体。` and require read-only `delivery-review`: no edits, outside contact, commitments or delegation. In that single dispatch, instruct the reviewer to form its own criteria and plausible material interpretations from `RAW EVIDENCE` before opening `HISTORY FILE`, then inspect the full record and return grounded counterexamples, repair directions, a delivery verdict and separate explanation errors.

## Parent repair and release

- **Repair from evidence.** Treat a specific evidence-grounded reviewer BLOCK as the default repair priority, not a competing opinion; verify its premises against original records. Contest it only with a pinpointed source-backed rebuttal or counterexample and independent re-review; never self-clear. A reviewer's proposed fix is not a new requirement or authorization. Repair the underlying judgment and affected dependents; if the approach fails or patches fragment the outcome, reconstruct a coherent result from the goal and evidence instead of retaining the draft's structure. Preserve supported substance. Before review or release, check each decisive statement or action for its purpose, evidence, authority and recipient-inferred meaning, then check that their combination serves the original goal without unsupported scope, certainty, specificity or commitment.
- **Deliver for use.** First establish how the supported facts, feasible paths, dependencies and decision rights fit into a coherent solution for the user's use point; then draft the smallest complete, evidence-backed, authorized result, including conditional options with prerequisites when requested or material to the goal, so unsupported commitments do not consume all review rounds. Lack of grounds for promising an outcome does not prevent truthful communication or authorized steps; consider compatible combinations and approval-dependent choices rather than false either/or alternatives. Include decisive facts, proportionate next actions and unmet conditions; do not substitute extra analysis or future plans for present completion. Where needed, explore and compare supported candidates by value and consequence; reject evidenced improper means, not merely disclosed risks. Keep unapproved options conditional and user-reserved trade-offs with the user. Check standalone readiness against material caveats; report noncompletion rather than invent commitments.
- **Respect review scope.** At most five dispatches per user turn, including retries; splitting, repair, or continuation never resets the count. A new user turn renews quota, not a prior BLOCK or a PASS for an old revision. Never re-sample an unchanged BLOCKed draft for another opinion.
- **Apply the result.** `PASS` permits release of only unchanged reviewed work after independent disposition of every applicable prior BLOCK by evidenced repair, authorized inapplicability, or source-backed refutation; it replaces neither tests nor authority. Passing individual checks or excerpts cannot clear a newly assembled artifact. `BLOCK` requires substantive repair/removal, new relevant evidence/authority, or a specific grounded rebuttal before re-review. `UNRESOLVED` withholds affected parts and dependents; consequential separable work still needs its own current PASS. At the five-dispatch limit, stop and report noncompletion or release only cleared work; never claim unperformed review or validation.
