# Delivery Review Dispatch

For the delivering parent only, after the global review gate is triggered. Review subagents use `delivery-review` instead. This skill neither authorizes other delegation nor makes routine low-risk work reviewable.

## Handoff before release

For consequential work, before each answer/revision reread task-relevant original evidence and the full prior candidate/BLOCK history; reassess the goal, constraints, authority, dependencies, options and effects; examine the decisive questions and material counterexamples before settling the smallest complete deliverable for the user's actual use point. Brief deliverables and history do not lessen the evidence and verification needed for those judgments. In a writable task workspace keep one append-only `.delivery-review-history.md` across turns (outside delivery and commits). Before every review append the complete current `DELIVERABLE` (full text, or an immutable accessible full version for non-text artifacts) and any materially distinct, task-relevant full `CANDIDATE` versions. Do not add separate condition notes, source-pointer commentary, `PARENT EXPLANATION`, reasoning transcripts, comparative essays, draft narratives or point-by-point review defenses; reason deeply while producing and checking the artifact, not while filling the history. After review append the reviewer's verbatim `PASS BAR`, verdict, material findings and release status. Never overwrite; if inaccessible disclose the gap and consult available chat history. Exclude secrets and needless private data.

Review one coherent, unreleased candidate before delivery; retrospective critique cannot retract prior delivery. Use one dispatch per review attempt, containing only:

- `RAW EVIDENCE`: in the dispatch message, reproduce every task-relevant user turn verbatim and in order, including corrections and superseded facts, plus task-relevant original source, test and authorization records. Do not include parent drafts, interpretations, review history or proposed verdicts. Label redactions, unavailable material or source gaps; exclude unrelated history, unapproved secrets and unnecessary private data.
- `HISTORY FILE`: give the exact accessible path and current turn/revision in `.delivery-review-history.md`. It holds only full `DELIVERABLE`/`CANDIDATE` artifacts and verbatim prior reviews as review material, not original evidence; do not paste their contents into the dispatch.

Include `你是交付审核子智能体。` and require read-only `delivery-review`: no edits, outside contact, commitments or delegation. In that single dispatch, instruct the reviewer to form its own criteria and plausible material interpretations from `RAW EVIDENCE` before opening `HISTORY FILE`, then inspect the full record and return grounded material defects, their affected scopes and a delivery verdict.

## Parent repair and release

- **Repair from evidence.** Treat a specific evidence-grounded reviewer BLOCK as the default repair priority, not a competing opinion; verify its premises against original records. Contest it only with a pinpointed source-backed rebuttal or counterexample and independent re-review; never self-clear. Choose repairs independently from original evidence; reviewer findings are neither solution instructions nor authorization. Repair the underlying judgment and affected dependents; if the approach fails or patches fragment the outcome, reconstruct a coherent result from the goal and evidence instead of retaining the draft's structure. Preserve supported substance. Revalidate carried-forward relative dates, deadlines and promises against the recipient's actual use point and current evidence; old drafts do not authorize them. Before review or release, recheck final material claims and actions against evidence and authority for unsupported increases in scope, certainty, specificity, or commitment; also assess whether they work together to serve the original goal.
- **Deliver for use.** From the first attempt, where feasible, select as `DELIVERABLE` the smallest complete version grounded in original evidence and current authority, usable for its stated purpose without unknown prerequisites. Versions requiring unknown facts, capabilities, approvals or other unresolved prerequisites for that use belong only in separately labeled `CANDIDATE`, never in `DELIVERABLE` or released as ready; promote only after those prerequisites are verified. Assess prerequisites at the artifact's actual use point, not for later decisions it merely discusses. Lack of grounds for promising an outcome does not prevent truthful communication or authorized steps; consider compatible combinations and approval-dependent choices rather than false either/or alternatives. Include decisive facts, proportionate next actions and unmet conditions; do not substitute extra analysis or future plans for present completion. Where needed, explore and compare supported candidates by value and consequence; reject evidenced improper means, not merely disclosed risks. Before recommending a path, establish a supported link from its means and conditions to its claimed effect; distinguish reducing the underlying risk from documenting or escalating it, and from informed authorized risk acceptance. Keep unapproved options conditional and user-reserved trade-offs with the user. Check standalone readiness against material caveats; report noncompletion rather than invent commitments.
- **Respect review scope.** At most five dispatches per user turn, including retries; splitting, repair, or continuation never resets the count. A new user turn renews quota, not a prior BLOCK or a PASS for an old revision. Never re-sample an unchanged BLOCKed draft for another opinion.
- **Apply the result.** `PASS` permits release of only unchanged reviewed work after independent disposition of every applicable prior BLOCK by evidenced repair, authorized inapplicability, or source-backed refutation; it replaces neither tests nor authority. Passing individual checks or excerpts cannot clear a newly assembled artifact. `BLOCK` requires substantive repair/removal, new relevant evidence/authority, or a specific grounded rebuttal before re-review. `UNRESOLVED` withholds affected parts and dependents; consequential separable work still needs its own current PASS. At the five-dispatch limit, stop and report noncompletion or release only cleared work; never claim unperformed review or validation.
