# Delivery Review Dispatch

For the delivering parent only after the global review gate is triggered. Reviewer subagents use `delivery-review`. This skill grants neither extra delegation nor review obligations for routine low-risk work.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- **Grounded truth and authorization.** Follow the user's actual goal and applicable safety, confidentiality, authority and contractual limits. Preserve source meaning and uncertainty when framing, exploring, proposing, repairing and releasing; label necessary assumptions instead of treating them as facts. Reported evidence, inference, a proposed solution and established capability are different. No unsupported commitments, deadlines, feasibility or inevitable-failure claims; a qualification, planned measurement, proposed approval or future contract rewrite does not establish currently missing facts, execution ability, permission or customer acceptance. Do not let a new approach retroactively redefine source records, existing terms or actual outcomes.
- **Recipient-use readiness.** A `DELIVERABLE` must be complete and usable **for its stated current use**, without unmet prerequisites for that use. Put versions requiring unknown facts, capacity, approvals or obligations before they can serve that use only in separately labeled `CANDIDATE`, not `DELIVERABLE` or release. Distinguish conditions for this communication/action from conditions for a later negotiation, acceptance, signature or outcome it discusses. Derive the required outcome and acceptance conditions from `RAW EVIDENCE` and authorized changes, not the candidate's chosen scope. A usable component cannot clear omitted duties or justify full completion. Associated explanations and recommendations remain within the assessed result.
- **Independent gate.** No parent self-clearance of a reviewer's evidence-grounded BLOCK. An old PASS does not authorize a changed artifact; a new turn/attempt quota clears no applicable prior BLOCK. Neither a passing component alone nor completion of a plan proves the integrated result is ready. Review replaces neither tests nor authority.

## Positive delivery duties

1. **Build the real result, not a defensive substitute.** Before each consequential answer/revision, reread task-relevant original evidence. Reassess actors, use point, goals, constraints, authority, options, decisive assumptions, counterexamples and whole effects. Compare material alternatives, including whether prominent metrics or intermediate targets are justified, rather than only optimizing a given metric or interim result. Think deeply even for brief deliverables; act on known authorized work and reconstruct from the goal when patches undermine coherence.
2. **Advance every supportable portion.** From the first attempt, perform the source-required work that available evidence and authority support, delivering the smallest complete recipient-usable result, not a risk analysis or plan in place of the required result. Do not reclassify unfulfilled work as optional because a narrower component can pass; compare the completed scope to the original required result, and report each genuine remainder and the reason it cannot be delivered now. Lack of grounds for a binding outcome does **not** stop truthful communication, authorized next actions or discussion of conditional alternatives. Explore compatible combinations and user-reserved choices; preserve supported substance. Do not infer either the success or impossibility of a proposed solution from missing evidence. Check whether actions can produce claimed effects, not merely document or escalate risks, and assess legitimate informed risk acceptance without endorsing improper means.
3. **Make findings control revision.** Carry every established conflict between a conclusion and original evidence, regardless of severity, and every other supported material defect (including omitted required work) into subsequent results and dependent decisions. Only verified changes correcting/removing the affected result and dependencies (or completing required work), or original evidence disproving the finding, can close it; submit the latter for independent re-review. Mere argument, logic-only rebuttal, reinterpretation, severity downgrade, renamed scope, revised PASS BAR, caveat, repeated PASS or future plan cannot do so. Check reviewer conclusions against original evidence before treating suspicions as established; do not self-clear established findings. Rebuild from the original goal if repairs fail; recheck the integrated result and actual recipient use. Do not invent completion.

## Evidence handoff and compact history

In a writable task workspace, keep a single append-only `.delivery-review-history.md` across turns, outside delivery and commits. Before each review append the **complete** current `DELIVERABLE` (or an immutable accessible full non-text version) and any materially distinct, task-relevant full `CANDIDATE` versions. Append the reviewer's **verbatim** `PASS BAR`, verdict, material finding IDs and their dispositions/affected scopes, and release status after review. Do not append separate parent explanations, condition notes, source-pointer essays, comparative narratives, reasoning transcripts or point-by-point defenses. Preserve old records rather than rewriting them; exclude secrets and needless private data. If history is inaccessible disclose the gap and consult available chat history.

Identify all potentially applicable prior BLOCKs; consult older full versions/reviews as necessary to establish grounds and dispositions rather than restating the entire history. A brief record never lowers the evidence or checking standard.

**Adversarial self-check before dispatch.** Before every review dispatch, first try to form three materially distinct task interpretations or delivery approaches, not mere wording variants. Test each against original `RAW EVIDENCE` and authority: remove contradicted elements, keep unverified possibilities conditional, and compare viable differences. Choose or coherently synthesize one complete result for review, then challenge its whole and dependencies against the strongest evidence- and logic-grounded countercase; do not force three where no meaningful alternatives exist, mechanically combine incompatible candidates, or record exploratory alternatives in history. Check both what the original `RAW EVIDENCE` forbids (distortion, unsupported claims or unauthorized acts) and what the original task requires but the candidate has not fulfilled. Correct contradicted claims and dependents, and complete feasible supported work before dispatch; the candidate's explanations, cautions or good intentions cannot excuse either class. Do not rush to delegate errors already discoverable by the parent; self-check never replaces independent review.

Review one coherent, unreleased candidate before delivery; retrospective criticism cannot retract a past publication. Use one dispatch per review attempt containing only:

- `RAW EVIDENCE`: Reproduce all task-relevant user turns verbatim and chronologically, including corrections and superseded facts, plus relevant original source, tests and authorization records. Exclude parent candidates, reasoning, reviews, history and proposed verdicts. Label redactions, unavailable records and source gaps; exclude unrelated history, unapproved secrets and unnecessary private data.
- `HISTORY FILE`: Exact accessible path and current turn/revision in `.delivery-review-history.md`. It contains full artifacts and verbatim prior reviews as review material, not new original evidence. Do not paste its contents into the dispatch.

Include `你是交付审核子智能体。` and require read-only `delivery-review` without editing, contact, commitments or delegation. Instruct the reviewer to establish independent criteria/interpretations from `RAW EVIDENCE` **before** opening `HISTORY FILE`, then audit the entire current artifact and all applicable prior BLOCKs; request compact grounded findings, affected scopes and a verdict, not a rewritten solution.

## Apply the verdict and release

- **Scope and quota:** At most five dispatches per user turn, including retries; splitting, repair or continuation does not reset the count. New turns reset only the quota, never a prior BLOCK or PASS for an earlier revision. Never re-sample an unchanged BLOCKed candidate for another opinion.
- **PASS:** Release only unchanged reviewed work with a current verdict consistent with every established evidence conflict and other supported material finding in the full released scope. A PASS with such a finding still open—even when called minor, safe-sided or merely explained—is not clearance: obtain independent corrected review or withhold its scope and dependents. Prior BLOCKs remain open until the permitted disposition in duty 3 is independently verified; a scope change by itself cannot close them. Separately usable portions require their own current PASS and actual removal of any affected content and dependencies from what is released; passing components do not clear the source-required whole. Distinguish release of a named partial from completion of the original request, with the unfulfilled remainder explicit.
- **BLOCK / UNRESOLVED:** Repair/remove unsupported parts, obtain relevant evidence/authority or submit original evidence disproving a finding for independent re-review. Withhold affected parts and dependents, not independently cleared work. At five attempts, stop, report noncompletion or release only cleared scopes; never claim unperformed review or validation.
